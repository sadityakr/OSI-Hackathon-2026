# 01: Join "Spectral Identifier" (Raman/FTIR material ID)

Team: Wilmer Gaspar Espinoza, Pushpender Kumar, Adewunmi Olaniyi (looking for teammates). Details in `resources/miro-pitch-spectral-identifier.md`.

## Problem
Identifying materials from Raman/FTIR spectra with transparent, reproducible, extensible open tools; existing tools are closed or hard to extend.

## Their approach
Mathematical descriptors (Ramanujan theta function + "GTSF") computed on spectra, matched against RRUFF; produce scientific reports.

## Where Aditya could add value
- Domain grounding: Raman of 2D materials and thin films, physical meaning of peaks, what a "good" identification report needs.
- Agentic pipeline: preprocessing, RRUFF retrieval, report generation with uncertainty, literature citations via MCP Bond.
- Evaluation: honest baselines (correlation matching, peak matching, embeddings) so novelty claims hold up.
