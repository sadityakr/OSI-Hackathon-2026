# Slack announcement: kickoff FAQ (Ben Blaiszik, #general, 2026-10-19)

Video: https://youtu.be/er9fraWmiFo

> We're fast approaching the Oct 21 start of the hackathon event!
>
> I'm unreasonably excited to see what everyone builds this year, and I think there's a real chance some of you 1) truly shock us with how you apply these powerful new capabilities or 2) produce paper-worthy discoveries. I recorded this video to answer some common questions about the event.

## tl;dr from the video

- **The challenge:** Models and harnesses have advanced a lot since last year's event (Fable, Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra, Gemini 4 Argon) vs Opus 4.1, GPT 5. Teams are encouraged to scope ambitiously and ask whether "two days to a real discovery" is now possible.
- **The mindset:** Speed over polish, learning over perfection, collaboration over silos.
- **Teams and topics:** Both are self-organized. Find teammates in the Slack teaming channel, on the Miro board, or at your site. Agree on a concept early and assign clear roles.
- **What's in scope:** Anything that meaningfully advances the physical sciences or mathematics. More than 120 examples are on the website: https://llmhackathon.github.io/submissions
- **Prizes:**
  - MDRI general prize, about $10K total
  - Sponsor prizes from Valency, Adaption and Marimo (you must use their platform)
  - An NVIDIA DGX Spark, subject to export controls
  - Other prizes TBD based on sponsorship
- **Resources:** Sponsor platform credits, curated datasets, and mentors on Slack and Zoom. Your own subscriptions and campus clusters are allowed.
- **Judging:** Potential for impact, innovativeness, relevance and novelty.
- **Timeline:**
  - Kickoff on Wed, Oct 21 (anywhere on Earth)
  - Submissions due Thu, Oct 22 at 7 pm CT / 5 pm PT (= **Fri Oct 23, 02:00 CEST**)
  - Winners announced within 1 to 2 weeks
  - Selected teams present at a virtual event in late November
- **How to submit:**
  1. Record a video of up to 4 minutes (don't speed up your voice) and write a one-page summary with a figure.
  2. Post the video publicly anywhere that doesn't require a login. A thread is fine.
  3. Fill out the submission form with your write-up, post link and code link.

Sponsors: UL Research Institutes / MDRI, Valency, NVIDIA, Hugging Face, Labric, Adaption, Marimo, Green Dynamics, NSF, NIST and Globus.
