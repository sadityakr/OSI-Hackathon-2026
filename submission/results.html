Literature-in-the-Loop: what the Raman skill changes

Raman spectra of 2D materials, analysed by Claude with and without the Raman skill (scripts, cited literature notes, and published figures fetched through iopic-Bond), compared with classical non-LLM methods. Bad results are shown next to good ones.

Findings at a glance

  • baseline
    Classical library search identifies 4 of 14 real 2D-material spectra (top-1 0.29, held out). The same matcher reaches 0.68 on synthetic spectra drawn from its own database, which is why synthetic scores are reported separately.
  • better
    Claims become traceable. Citation specificity (0 none, 1 paper named, 2 paper plus figure or table) rises from 1.00 to 1.57 on held-out interpretation and from 1.00 to 1.86 on development cases (Haiku 5.5).
  • worse
    Held-out interpretation score fell with skill v3: rubric 0.50 bare vs 0.41 with the skill (Haiku 5.5, n = 7). Two of the losses came from the skill's own tooling (a series fit killed by a time limit, a case that hit the 30 min limit), and no v3 run opened a single paper.
  • better
    With literature in the loop (v4), the agent opens the right published figure on its own in 5 of 5 dry runs, never touches the withheld source papers, and writes a notebook that shows the figure beside its own fits.
  • demo
    CrSBr thickness: the notebook's per-band fits order four of six flakes as AFM does; the two thinnest are measured correctly but left unassigned. CrSBr after He+ irradiation: the agent quantifies the loss of crystalline signal step by step and rejects the flake for devices.

Skill v4 · literature in the loop · Claude Sonnet 5.5 dry runs

The agent fetches the published figure it needs

Skill v4 adds one required step: name the variable the data span (thickness, dose, temperature) and open a published figure that shows that variable for the material. iopic-Bond downloads the arXiv paper once, extracts its figures, and hands the image to the model. The source paper of each dataset is withheld: iopic-Bond refuses it by arXiv id, DOI and title.

What went right

  • Ag1 measured for all six flakes, including the two weak ones the full-spectrum fit merged.
  • A < F < D < B, the AFM order.
  • Ranked by the Ag2−Ag1 spacing because no Si line was in the window, so a calibration offset cancels.

What fell short

  • C and E left unassigned: their Ag1 lies below the 1L value read from a figure measured at 1.58 eV, while these data are at 2.33 eV.
  • Bare Claude measured the same shifts but ranked by signal strength; skill v3 ranked A thinnest. v4 was written after v3 failed this case, so this is a demonstration.

CrSBr after three processing steps: still good for a device?

One 4-layer flake, measured untreated and after three He+ irradiation doses (3.9×1013, 2.4×1014, 1.2×1015 cm−2), with the laser along the a and the b axis. The agent was told only "processing steps".

Band area ÷ Si 520 cm−1 areauntreatedstep 1step 2step 3
Ag2, laser along a0.4660.1710.075< 0.001
Ag3, laser along b0.3300.1240.046< 0.001
  • Verdict: not device-ready. Intensity down 6 to 7 times after step 2, lines twice as broad (about 3.7 to 7 cm−1), no CrSBr modes after step 3.
  • Confirmed the untreated flake as 4L CrSBr against Mondal et al. 2025 (Ag modes 113.8, 246.0, 344.6 vs 114, 244, 344 cm−1) and checked the a/b polarization pattern.
  • Separated what could be calibration (the untreated spectrum was taken with different settings) from the like-for-like steps 1 to 2.
  • Compared the red-side shoulders with the published defect figures (Torres 2022, Figs. 2 and 3) and kept that reading at low confidence; asked what each step was instead of guessing.

How to read these numbers

  • Fair-test rule: the agent never reads the paper its test data come from. The skill notes were written without those papers, and iopic-Bond withholds them at run time. In the scored v3 runs the skill arm also had direct Valency search, which cannot be filtered; the iopic-Bond guard came later.
  • Same model, prompt and Python environment in both arms; each case in a fresh folder, web tools and sub-agents off.
  • The judge is a language model, mitigated by paper-derived rubrics and blinding; interpretation truth is each paper's own reading.
  • Experimental cases are few (14 identification, 7 interpretation); the held-out Sonnet runs and the held-out LLM identification runs were not completed.
