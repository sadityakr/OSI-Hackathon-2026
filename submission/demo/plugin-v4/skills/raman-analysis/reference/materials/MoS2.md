# MoS2: expert Raman notes

Scope: 2H-MoS2 (and the 3R polytype where noted). Numbers appear only where they were read in the cited source;
the locator says where. "Consensus" means several independent sources agree; "single source" means one paper.
Positions move 1 to 3 cm-1 with calibration, substrate, strain and doping: reason from differences and trends.

## Signature and irreps
- Bulk 2H (D6h): Raman-active A1g, E1g, E2g (two); E1g is forbidden in backscattering from the basal plane
  (Zhang X. et al. 2015, Chem. Soc. Rev. 44, 2757, arXiv:1502.00701, p.3 and p.6).
- Monolayer (D3h): E2g1 becomes E', A1g becomes A1'; odd N is D3h, even N is D3d, so labels change with parity
  (Zhang X. 2015, p.3, p.7).
- Main lines at 514.5 nm on 285 nm SiO2/Si: 1L E' 384.0, A1' 403.1 cm-1 (separation 19.1); bulk 382.2 / 407.4
  (25.2) (Lee C. et al. 2010, ACS Nano 4, 2695, arXiv:1005.2509, Fig. 2b). Consensus.
- Widths: E2g1 about 2.1 cm-1; A1g 3 to 6 cm-1, broadening from 6L to 2L (Lee 2010, p.4).
- Bulk interlayer shear mode E2g2 near 32 cm-1 (Zhang X. 2015, p.11).

## Thickness
- E2g1 softens and A1g stiffens with N; the separation is the standard layer counter up to about 4L
  (Lee 2010, Fig. 2b). It holds on SiO2, hBN, mica, Gel-Film, Au and few-layer graphene (Buscema M. et al. 2014,
  arXiv:1311.3869, p.4). Consensus.
- Above about 5L the separation saturates; use the low-frequency shear (C) and layer-breathing (LB) modes, whose
  positions follow a linear-chain model with different branches for odd and even N
  (Zhang X. et al. 2013, PRB 87, 115413, arXiv:1212.6796, abstract and Fig. 2; review Fig. 5, p.8).
- Stacking: 2H vs 3R bilayers differ in the low-frequency modes, not in the E/A pair
  (Ranjuna M. K. et al. 2023, Sci. Rep. 13, 16418, doi:10.1038/s41598-023-43756-w, p.2, Fig. 1d to f).
- Intensity is not a thickness measure on its own: MoS2 and Si intensities oscillate with N through optical
  interference; the Si-normalized MoS2 signal is monotonic and can be used (Li S.-L. et al., arXiv:1308.3835,
  abstract; companion of ACS Nano 6, 7381, 2012).

## Strain and doping
- Uniaxial tensile strain: E' softens about -4.5 cm-1/% (1L) and splits into E'+ and E'-; A1' shows no
  measurable shift (Conley H. J. et al. 2013, Nano Lett. 13, 3626, arXiv:1305.3880, text). Consensus.
- Electron doping (electrolyte gate): 1L A1g softens about 4 cm-1 and broadens about 6 cm-1 at 1.8e13 cm-2;
  E2g1 moves about 0.6 cm-1 (Chakraborty B. et al. 2012, PRB 85, 161403, arXiv:1206.1758, text).
- Rule of thumb: A1' shift with broadening points to doping; E' shift, especially with splitting, to strain.
- Substrate doping: A1g of 1L stiffens by up to about 2 cm-1 from SiO2 (lowest) to hBN (highest) while E2g1
  barely moves, attributed to lower electron density on hBN (Buscema 2014, p.7, Fig. 4c).
- Transfer can shift peaks through strain release and interface effects (Zhang X. et al. 2025, Nat. Commun. 16,
  4468, doi:10.1038/s41467-025-59803-1, p.4); monolayer separation is "typically about 20 cm-1" there.

## Defects and quality
- LA(M) near 227 cm-1 is the main defect-activated band; I(LA)/I(first order) scales with defect density
  (Mignuzzi S. et al. 2015, PRB 91, 195411, abstract). Quantitative L_D calibration not verified here.
- Few-layer modes near 286 and 471 cm-1 (forbidden for 1L backscattering) are intrinsic resonant modes, not
  defects; strongest at UV excitation such as 3.8 eV (Scheuschner N. et al. 2015, PRB 91, 235409,
  arXiv:1503.08980, pp.8 to 9).
- Sulfur vacancies show up more in widths and relative intensities than in positions at high temperature
  (Ranjuna 2023, p.9). Narrow lines (E2g1 about 2 cm-1, Lee 2010) indicate good crystals.

## Temperature and heating
- Both main modes redshift on heating. First-order coefficients: few-layer -1.32e-2 (E2g1) and -1.23e-2 (A1g)
  cm-1/K (Sahoo S. et al. 2013, J. Phys. Chem. C, doi:10.1021/jp402509w, arXiv:1302.5865, p.6); 1L
  -0.013 (E2g) and -0.016 (A1g) cm-1/K (Lanzillo N. et al. 2013, APL, doi:10.1063/1.4819337, Table I);
  1L on SiO2 is nonlinear below room temperature, literature values -0.011 to -0.017 cm-1/K
  (Taube A. et al. 2014, ACS AMI, doi:10.1021/am502359k, Table 1, p.5). Consensus on sign and order; values
  scatter with substrate and sample.
- Laser heating redshifts and broadens both modes (Ranjuna 2023, pp.5 to 6, Fig. 6). On a suspended few-layer
  flake A1g shifts about 5.4 cm-1/mW (Sahoo 2013, p.10): suspended flakes heat strongly.
- Damage: 10 mW at 514 nm, scanned, sublimates upper layers of multilayer MoS2 on SiO2 and leaves a monolayer,
  because the bottom layer is heat-sunk to the substrate (Castellanos-Gomez A. et al. 2012, Nano Lett. 12, 3187,
  arXiv:1206.4323, p.3).
- Modest laser exposure in air can also increase Raman intensity 3 to 5 times, harden A1g by 1.4 cm-1 and soften
  E2g1 by 1 cm-1 (attributed to defect healing) (Bera A. et al. 2017, arXiv:1707.09386, abstract). So repeated
  scans of one spot are not independent.

## Excitation wavelength and resonance
- 633 nm (near the A exciton) gives resonant spectra rich in second-order bands that evolve with N: 2LA(M)
  near 450, A1g(M)-LA(M) near 179, a B1u Davydov partner of A1g near 403 that grows with N, and a 420 cm-1
  band (B2g2+E1u-type combination) that is absent in 1L (Zhang X. 2015, Fig. 12, pp.13 to 14; 1L assignments
  in Table III, p.15). Off resonance (514, 532 nm) the
  spectrum is mainly E' and A1'.
- UV (325 nm) enhances combination modes 750 to 840 cm-1 (Zhang X. 2015, p.15).
- Consequence: peak lists and intensity ratios are only comparable at the same laser line.

## Polarization and substrate
- Backscattering, linear: A1g/A1' appears in XX and vanishes in YX; E2g1/E' is present in both with equal
  intensity (Zhang X. 2015, p.6 to 7, Fig. 3c). Use this to separate overlapping modes.
- Substrate also modulates intensity through interference (Buscema 2014, abstract; Li arXiv:1308.3835).
- Mica has a Bg mode that can overlap A1g (Buscema 2014, p.7).

## Pitfalls and artifacts
- Spectral resolution: Lee 2010 used 2.7 cm-1; the 1L-2L separation step is about 2 cm-1, comparable.
- 3R and 1T polytypes break the 2H rules (see materials.md); check stacking before applying the separation.
- Calibrate with Si (about 520 cm-1) in the same spectrum; report the laser line.
- Doping and strain both move A1': a single spectrum cannot separate all causes; say so.

## What to ask
- Laser wavelength, power at the sample, spot size, objective; was power varied?
- Substrate (SiO2 thickness), suspended or supported, encapsulated (hBN), transferred or as-grown.
- Exfoliated or CVD; age and storage; any gating or illumination history.
- Polarization configuration; temperature and environment (air, vacuum).
- Grating, resolution and calibration reference.
