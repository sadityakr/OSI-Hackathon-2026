**Caption:** FIG. 2. Raman modes polarization switching at different laser energies. All results are obtained from a fourmonolayer (4L) thick CrSBr sample. (a, b) Raman spectra at T = 300 K under an excitation laser energy of (a) EL = 2.33 eV and (b) EL = 1.96 eV for polarization along the b axis (top) and a axis (bottom). (c, d) Color mapping of Raman scattering signal for (c) EL = 2.33 eV and (d) EL = 1.96 eV as a function of polarization orientation angle, a and b axes are indicated as horizontal dotted lines and correspond to the individual spectra shown in (a, b). Under excitation of 2.33 eV A2g mode reveals the polarization along a direction rotated by 90 degrees concerning A1g and A3g modes polarized along b axis. While under 1.96 eV all phonon modes are polarized along the b axis.
