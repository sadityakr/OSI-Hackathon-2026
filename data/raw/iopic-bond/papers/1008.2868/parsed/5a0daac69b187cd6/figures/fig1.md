**Caption:** FIG 1. (Color online) Atomically thin BN on top of an oxidized Si wafer (290 nm of SiO2) as seen in an optical microscope using a yellow filter (λ =590 nm). The central crystal is a monolayer. For legibility, the contrast is enhanced by a factor of 2. The insets show AFM images of the 3.5x3.5μm 2 regions indicated by the squares.
