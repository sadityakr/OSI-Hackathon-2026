**Caption:** SI Fig.| 11. Development of surface related defect mode D1 in a 3L CrSBr. a, Raman spectra of CrSBr 3L over a period of 29 days submerged in water as exfoliated and b, heated to 75 °C for 5 minutes after exfoliation. c Raman spectra of CrSBr 3L over a period of several days left in ambient as exfoliated and d heated to 105 °C for 2 minutes. e, the area of the D1 mode divided over the area of the A1g mode as a function of time submerged in water (corresponding to flakes in a, and b) or left in air (corresponding to flakes in c, and d) For each flake, there is a positive linear relationship between the area ratio as a function of time, with the slopes listed on the plot. The slope is significantly higher for flakes submerged in water versus those left in air, indicating that submerging in water accelerates the degradation process and defect formation.
