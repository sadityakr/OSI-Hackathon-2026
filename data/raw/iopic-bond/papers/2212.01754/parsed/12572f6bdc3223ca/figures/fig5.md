**Caption:** FIG.| 5. Pronounced spin-phonon coupling in pristine and He+ irradiated 3L CrSBr. a, Contour plot of the Raman temperature evolution of 3L CrSBr collected at EL = 1.58 eV. The dashed horizontal lines mark the Curie (TC ∼ 160K) and Néel temperature (TN ∼ 134K). b, Corresponding waterfall representation. c, Contour plot of the Raman temperature evolution of He+ irradiated 3L CrSBr at a dose of σ = 8 · 1014 cm-2 . d, Corresponding waterfall representation. e, A1g mode intensity of pristine and irradiated material as a function of temperature. The antiferromagnetic (AFM), intermediate ferromagnetic (iFM) and paramagnetic (PM) regime are marked. f-h, Temperature evolution of the mode position of A1g, A2g and A3g for pristine and He+ irradiated CrSBr. i, Temperature dependent intensity of the He+ induced D3 defect mode.
