**Caption:** FIG.| 4. Resonantly enhanced defect modes in He+ irradiated 4L CrSBr. a, False color contour plot of colinearly polarized Raman of irradiated 4L CrSBr from 0-360 degrees for resonant excitation at EL = 1.58 eV. Angles of 0, 180 and 360 degrees correspond to the b axis while 90 and 270 degree correspond to the a axis. The irradiation dose is 2 · 1014 cm-2 . b, Selected Raman spectra from 0 to 90 degrees. c, False color contour plot of co-linearly polarized Raman of irradiated 4L CrSBr from 0-360 degrees for nonresonant excitation at EL = 2.33 eV. The irradiation dose is 2·1014 cm-2 . d, Selected Raman spectra from 0 to 90 degrees. e, Polar plot of the A3g and the D3 mode showing polarization along the b axis with a high degree of polarization of ρ ∼ 99%, respectively. f, Polar plot for non-resonant excitation shows a polarization along the b axis with a high degree of polarization for the A3g mode but a low one for the D3 mode (ρ ∼ 49%).
