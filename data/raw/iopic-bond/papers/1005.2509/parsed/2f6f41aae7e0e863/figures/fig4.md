**Caption:** FIG. 4. (Color online) (a) Optical micrograph of single (1L) and few-layer (>5L) MoS2 films placed on a patterned substrate with 1-and1.5-µm diameter holes. Raman measurements were carried out on both the supported and suspended portions of such samples. (b) Frequencies of E 1 2g and A1g Raman modes as a function of layer thickness for MoS2 samples supported on the substrate (blue triangles) and suspended over the holes (red circles). For comparison, the corresponding data for MoS2 samples prepared on unpatterned substrates (from Fig. 2b, measured at a slightly different wavelength) are shown as open black squares.
