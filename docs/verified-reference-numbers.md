## MoS2 defects (Mignuzzi et al. 2015, PRB 91, 195411)
LA(M) at about 227 cm-1, the most prominent defect-activated peak; I(LA)/I(first order) proportional to defect density (abstract, text). L_D constants NOT verified.

## MoS2 strain and doping
- Conley et al. 2013, Nano Lett. 13, 3626, arXiv 1305.3880 (532 nm, uniaxial 0 to 2.2 %): E'- -4.5+-0.3 (1L), -4.6+-0.4 (2L) cm-1/%; E'+ -1.0+-1 (1L); A' no measurable shift; unstrained A' 403, E' 384. (text)
- Chakraborty et al. 2012, PRB 85, 161403, arXiv 1206.1758 (514.5 nm, electrolyte gate): 1L A1g softens 4 cm-1 and broadens 6 cm-1 at n = 1.8e13 cm-2 electrons; E2g1 shifts about 0.6 cm-1. (text)

## MoSe2, WSe2
- MoSe2 bulk (Nam et al. 2015, arXiv 1511.01225, pp.4-5): A1g 242, E2g1 285, E1g 169 (resonant). 1L NOT verified. (text)
- WSe2 bulk (Sahin et al. 2013, arXiv 1303.5861, p.3): degenerate A1g/E2g peak at 252.2 cm-1 (90 nm SiO2/Si); extra peak 5, 6, 11 cm-1 above the main peak for 3L, 2L, 1L (p.4). (text)

## hBN (Gorbachev et al. 2011, Small 7, 465, arXiv 1008.2868; 514.5 nm, SiO2/Si)
Bulk E2g about 1366 cm-1, HWHM about 9; 1L upshifted typically 2 to 4 cm-1 (sample dependent +-2), HWHM 10 to 12; 2L usually downshifted 1 to 2 cm-1. (text)

## CrSBr
- Mondal et al. 2025, npj 2D Mater., arXiv 2410.22164, p.2 (4L, 300 K, 2.33 and 1.96 eV, 85 nm SiO2/Si): Ag1 114, Ag2 244, Ag3 344. (text)
- Pawbake et al. 2023, PRB 107, 075421, arXiv 2211.12939, p.3 (bulk, 633 nm): Ag 117, 257, 345 at room temperature; 121, 254, 346 at 5 K; B2g 92, 194, 301 only with a 60 deg tilted sample. (text)

## Black phosphorus (Castellanos-Gomez et al. 2014, arXiv 1403.0499, p.7; 514 nm)
Ag1 362.1, B2g 439.5, Ag2 467.7 (1.6 to 9 nm flakes); bulk literature 362.5, 439.8, 467.1. Si 520.9 in the same setup. (text)

## Graphene
- Ferrari et al. 2006, PRL 97, 187401, arXiv cond-mat/0606284, p.2 (514 nm): G about 1580, 2D about 2700; graphene G 3 to 5 cm-1 above bulk graphite. (text)
- Mohiuddin et al. 2009, PRB 79, 205433, arXiv 0812.1538 (514 nm, uniaxial, Poisson 0.33): G+ -10.8, G- -31.7, 2D -64, 2D' -35 cm-1/%; biaxial predictions G about -63, 2D about -191 cm-1/%. (text)
- Lee et al. 2012, Nat. Commun. 3, 1024, arXiv 1209.0234: origin (1581.6, 2676.9); strain slope 2.2+-0.2; hole-doping slope 0.70+-0.05; 514.5 nm. (text, research agent)
- Cancado et al. 2011, Nano Lett. 11, 3190, arXiv 1105.0175: L_D^2 (nm^2) = (1.8+-0.5)e-9 lambda^4 (I_D/I_G)^-1; n_D (cm-2) = (1.8+-0.5)e22 / lambda^4 (I_D/I_G); heights; L_D >= 10 nm. (text)
- Eckmann et al. 2012, Nano Lett. 12, 3925, arXiv 1207.2058: I_D/I_D' about 13 sp3, 7 vacancy, 3.5 boundary. (abstract)

## Silicon
First order 520 (Lee 2010), 520.9 (Castellanos-Gomez 2014, 514 nm). Second order: experimental features near 950 and 980 cm-1, plus 1030 only at 3.41 eV (Gillet et al. 2017, arXiv 1708.05874, Fig. 1, reproducing Renucci et al.). 2TA near 300 cm-1 from DFPT only (Gillet Fig. 4). Experimental 2TA NOT verified.
